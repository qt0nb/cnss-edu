Course Aim

To equip students with the knowledge and skills to design, configure, and
manage scalable, secure SD-WAN architectures. Students will learn to
transition from traditional WANs to SD-WAN, deploy and configure WAN
Edge devices, apply intelligent policies for performance and security, and
troubleshoot real-world networking challenges.

Learning Outcome Description
