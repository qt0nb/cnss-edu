Course Aim
To provide students with an understanding of what information security is and its value in modern technology. Students will gain a detailed
knowledge of information security through practical skills and analysis of the security requirements of organisations.

Learning Outcome Description

1

Demonstrate detailed knowledge of the principles of information security and state measures to secure information.

2

Develop an information security policy to demonstrate security awareness.

3

Perform and report upon an ethical penetration test.

4

Demonstrate detailed knowledge of cryptographic methods.

5

Identify and describe security implications for modern networks
