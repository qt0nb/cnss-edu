Course Aim
This course develops students’ advanced English communication skills for undergraduate study through data interpretation, survey communication,
academic writing, presentations, and responsible use of digital tools including AI.

CILO
On Completion of this course, the learner will be able to

Learning Outcome Description

1

Analyze complex written, spoken, visual and data - based information using appropriate communication strategies in academic and workplace contexts.

2

Design survey questions appropriate to purpose, audience, and research context.

3

Interpret charts and graphical data to draw justified conclusions in oral and written forms.

4

Use digital tools, including AI, to support the development of communication outputs, with awareness of ethical considerations and limitations.
