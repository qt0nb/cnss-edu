Course Aim
This course equips IT students with the practical experience to plan, manage, and execute real-world projects aligned with the UN Sustainable
Development Goals (UN SDGs). It also prepares students for the globally recognized CAPM certification by reinforcing key project management
principles and terminology based on PMI standards.

Learning Outcome Description

1

Use project management principles, tools, and techniques in the design of collaborative IT projects aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs),
using frameworks consistent with CAPM® standards.

2

Organize structured project documentation for stakeholder-oriented presentations.

3

Present innovative IT-based solutions, with potential social and sustainable impact.
