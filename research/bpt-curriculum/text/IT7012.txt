Course Aim
The course aims to equip students with essential entrepreneurial principles and research methodologies to support innovation in technical and
practical contexts. Students will first explore how to identify and evaluate opportunities for product, service, or venture development. They will then
learn how to design and conduct applied research to validate and refine their ideas. Emphasizing both evidence-based decision-making and
entrepreneurial thinking, the course prepares students to engage with real-world challenges, supporting diverse research, career and innovation
pathways.
