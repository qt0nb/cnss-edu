Course Aim
To provide students with skills in building LANs, VLANs and WANs using networking technology.
Students will configure local networks and manage switches. Students obtain skills in Wifi networks and access point configuration for Wifi networks.
Students are introduced to WAN protocols and build a WAN using different WAN’s technologies and protocols.
Also, Students are introduced to different IP addressing services and network device security.

Learning Outcome Description

1

Demonstrate advanced knowledge of the required protocols for VLAN implementation

2

Implement and troubleshoot the VLAN Trunking Protocol (VTP), Inter-VLAN Routing and Spanning Tree Protocol (STP)

3

Demonstrate advanced knowledge of the required protocols for WAN implementation

4

Plan and configure LAN and WAN network connections and differentiate between WAN and LAN equipment and protocols

5

Design a network security policy by using Access Control Lists (ACLs) and IP addressing services to implement this policy
