Learning Outcome Description

1

Apply appropriate statistical techniques to solve problems in defined and some undefined ICT scenarios.

2

Use some advanced geometrical methods in simulation applications.

3

Apply basic and some advanced number theory principles to solve a variety of problems in ICT context.

4

Solve cryptography-related problems which are linked to information security.
